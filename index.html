Four steps from the road to the plug

No more circling the car park. The bay is guarded from the moment you book until your car is the one standing in it.

  1. 01

    Find

    Search chargers on the map and pick one. Every charger can be booked through Baywatch.

  2. 02

    Book

    Pick a day up to 60 days ahead, then a start and a length in 15-minute blocks. The guard keeps the bay free for you.

  3. 03

    Arrive

    The camera reads your plate and lowers the guard. You can arrive up to 15 minutes after your start.

  4. 04

    Charge

    Plug in as usual. When you drive off, the bay is free for the next driver.

When the bay doesn’t play along, you’re covered

  • Someone parked in your bay

    The operator is alerted, you see the nearest free chargers and you get €5 credit. Cancelling is free, and if the bay clears, you get the lost time back.

  • The camera misreads your plate

    Dirt, glare and look-alike characters (B and 8, O and 0) still match your car. If nothing reads, tap “I’m at the bay”.

  • You’re in a different car today

    Add today’s plate to the booking. It’s forgotten as soon as you leave.
